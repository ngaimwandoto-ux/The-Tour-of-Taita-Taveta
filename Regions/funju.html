Leg 02 · Funju

Sat 17 – Mon 19 Oct 2026  ·  Date confirmed
The Route

Course & profile

Funju's route winds through the Taita highlands, passing chilli farms, traditional Taita houses, and scenic viewpoints. The signature stretch includes the rocky terrain above Funju village with panoramic views across the county. Route maps for this leg are finalised and reconfirmed with the county and local communities before each edition — check back closer to race day for the exact GPX file and road closures.
Tourism & Culture

What there is to see

Beyond the finish line, Funju has its own reasons to linger — from traditional Taita houses to a living agricultural community.

Conservation

Why the terrain is protected

Funju's forests and highland ecosystems are part of the Taita Hills — a biodiversity hotspot recognised in county planning for ecotourism development.

Flora & Fauna

Species and plants of note

The Funju highlands are home to diverse plant species, including those used in traditional medicine and agriculture.

Gastronomy

Food, drink & culinary heritage

From chilli-based dishes to honey harvested from local apiaries — taste the flavours of Funju.

Traditional Medicine

Healing plants & indigenous knowledge

Generations of Taita knowledge in plant-based remedies, passed down through community elders.
